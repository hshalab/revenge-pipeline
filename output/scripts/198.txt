I am not the kind of woman you notice. My late husband Marcus was the quiet architect of our family, and he believed that orchard would hold our grandchildren someday. The death hit me like a dull blade. Marcus had gone out to the south pasture to move a tractor hitch, and the hitch failed, and he was gone before the ambulance reached the gravel road. The day after Marcus's funeral, I went into his workshop to find a single object he had always kept on the shelf above the cider press.
