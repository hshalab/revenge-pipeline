I want the record to show that the man who tried to forge his mother out of her life's work is now spending his days stitching paper back together, one honest stitch at a time. He does not speak much, and he avoids my eyes, but he works. That is enough for now.

Every morning, I open the shop and set the Geneva Bible on a stand by the front counter, with the hollow board open and the original agreement framed on the wall above it. Paul's warning is still on the last page. Do not trust Daniel. Save yourself. I read it and I think about how he had seen the rot before I did, and how he had built a dead man's switch out of old paper and a bank officer's integrity. He knew I would never open that Bible until I was ready, and he knew Daniel would never open it at all. That was the whole trick. He used their arrogance as a hiding place. Integrity is not inherited. It is stitched one choice at a time, and when someone shows you who they are, believe them the first time.
